What the learner should understand

Allele frequency

Track an allele's proportion in a population over time.

Prerequisite: None listed

Evidence of understanding:

  • Describe a change in allele frequency.

02

Genetic drift

Recognize variation among stochastic population runs.

Prerequisite: Allele frequency

Evidence of understanding:

  • Compare repeated runs under the same settings.

03

Selection and comparison

Compare repeated runs with and without a fitness difference.

Prerequisite: Genetic drift

Evidence of understanding:

  • Explain why individual trajectories can differ.
