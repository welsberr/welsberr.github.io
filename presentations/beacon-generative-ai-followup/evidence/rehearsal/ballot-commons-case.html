From an address to a bounded claim

A real, historical case and a separate fictional test show how the workflow exposes what it knows and what remains uncertain.

1 · The real case · October 2026

Trace the lookup

Presenter-provided address: 1273 Divine Dr, Eaton Rapids, MI 48827. The presenter authorized its use in this talk.

  1. The recorded address-only Michigan voter-information lookup returned the Fire Department polling location.
  2. The recorded county polling-location list identified that location as Eaton Rapids Township Precinct 1.
  3. A public state preview for that precinct supplied a contest list for the November 3, 2026 election.

What was established: the recorded lookup supports a precinct-matched preview. What was not established: the voter-record sample ballot was not retrieved. The precinct preview displayed two school-board sections; the address-only result did not identify which school district applied.

This is a dated research example, not current voting guidance. The source is the presenter’s private report, summarized here with permission; the full report and voter identifiers are not included in the presentation. The former county PDF link returned 404 and the state preview returned an access challenge during a later automated check, so the historical lookup is reported from the saved account rather than presented as a new live confirmation.

2 · Software beyond the assistant

Inspect the controls

Ballot Commons provides structured source and claim records, a jurisdiction check, an evidence ledger, and review rules. Its own code distinguishes a candidate’s assertion from an official fact and can keep unresolved claims visible. The available record does not demonstrate that ClaimWright, GroundRecall, or another named stack component ran these particular checks.

3 · Reproducible fictional check

One pass and one stop

On 20 November 2026, the repository’s fictional example passed bundle validation and the jurisdiction check: its two synthetic sources agreed. In a separate strict ledger check, two synthetic claims were marked verified and one candidate-position claim required human review. Strict mode returned a blocking result.

The fictional sources and claims are test data. Their passing results do not certify the real precinct preview or establish that a human reviewed a real candidate claim.
