2 · CiteGeist

Resolve an actual reference

The topic’s BibTeX includes Hodgson and Baker’s 1967 article, “Porphyrin Abiogenesis from Pyrrole and Formaldehyde under Simulated Geochemical Conditions.” A separate CiteGeist check on 20 November resolved its DOI, 10.1038/216029a0, to an exact bibliographic identity via Crossref.

Positive result: the lookup returned authors, title, venue, year, volume, issue, and pages. Boundary: this identifies a reference; it does not establish that the article supports a particular scientific claim. This demonstration lookup did not modify the public bibliography.

3 · Translation and publication

Coordinate the next stage

The documented bibliography workflow renders CiteGeist topic data as HTML and BibTeX and places new pages into four-language translation work orders. The translation system records terminology and work-unit state and supports retries and recovery.

Positive result: the workflow produces reusable outputs and traceable work orders. Boundary: a generated translation or queue status does not establish expert translation quality. The linked workflow record describes an inspection dated 25 September 2026; check live status separately.
